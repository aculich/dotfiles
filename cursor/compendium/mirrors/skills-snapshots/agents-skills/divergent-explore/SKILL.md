# Divergent explore

Generate **option volume** with a timer on critique.

## When to Use

- Brainstorming collapses to two polar choices.
- The group anchors on the first acceptable idea.
- You need provocations to escape local maxima.

## Workflow

1. **Restate constraints** from [survey-creative-landscape](../survey-creative-landscape/SKILL.md) in ≤5 bullets.
2. **Silent brainstorm** — aim for N≥15 micro-ideas (headlines only).
3. **Provocation passes** — apply two lenses from SCAMPER (substitute, combine, adapt, modify, put to other uses, eliminate, reverse) or a deliberate “opposite day” constraint flip.
4. **Cluster** — affinity-group headlines without debating merit yet.
5. **Harvest** — mark top 3 weird-but-teachable clusters for [envision-end-state](../envision-end-state/SKILL.md) to story-tell and stress-test.

## Principles

- **Defer convergence**: no scoring until step 5.
- **Quantity before quality** is a tactic, not a moral law—timebox it.
- Ideas that violate hard constraints get a “parking lot / needs law review” tag, not instant death.
